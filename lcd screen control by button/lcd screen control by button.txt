Display Pin -------> Pi Pico pin
GND --> GND
VCC --> 3V3 OUT
SCL --> GP18
RES --> GP19
DC --> GP15
CS --> GP17
BLK --> 3V3 OUT (if one wire is not enough , use two for BLK)
